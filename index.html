The map is made up: a node's spot on it has nothing to do with where it really is. Pets show here only by their generated names. Nothing anyone types, no pictures, no locations, no accounts, and no cookies or tracking. Boards update when nodes report in. Dashed markers are virtual nodes: pets reach them over home Wi-Fi. Every night from 12 to 5am Pacific, a virus takes over a few of them (green); beat its boss once to cure one.
Activity is recorded by day, so the smallest view is Day.
