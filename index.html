The Grid is made up: a node's spot on it has nothing to do with where it really is. Pets show here only by their generated names. Nothing anyone types, no pictures, no locations, no accounts, and no cookies or tracking. Boards update when nodes report in.

Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a node for its boards. Keys: + / − zoom, 0 fit, Esc back.
