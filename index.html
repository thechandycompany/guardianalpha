    The map is made up: a node's spot on it has nothing to do with where it really is. Guardians show here only by their generated names. Nothing anyone types, no locations, no accounts, and no cookies or tracking. Boards update when nodes report in. Dashed markers are virtual nodes: Guardians reach them over home Wi-Fi. Every night from 12 to 5am Pacific, an infection takes over a few of them (green); beat its virus boss once to clean it out. Gold stars in marching gold frames are the Super Gateways (North, East, South and West), with how much of the ring a faction holds. They open only to a faction whose champions hold all 8 nodes around one. Their bosses are much tougher and their finds much rarer, and beating one infects all 8 nodes around it. Stronger nodes (champion level plus boss level) have brighter tiles and bigger, brighter markers, with up to 3 pips. The marks on the tiles (peaks, trees, water, dunes, grass) come from each node's name.
    Activity is recorded by day, so the smallest view is Day.
